### Orbit, your project's space

Every project gets its own private space in [Orbit](https://elevenbeyond.com/orbit). It opens the
moment you write to us, with your note as its first message, and stays with the project to launch.

- **Where it stands**: the stage we're in and the next step, always marked
- **Every week**: a written update while we build, also sent to your inbox
- **Waiting on you**: the decisions and materials we need, with dates
- **In one place**: the brief, the designs, test builds and files, a conversation with the team,
  stage sign-offs with a record, and invoices with a way to pay
- **No passwords**: sign in with a code by email, or with Google

[Open your orbit →](https://elevenbeyond.com/orbit)

**Delivering your code here.** When we hand over a project, the code and docs reach you the way you
choose in Orbit: pushed to your own GitHub repository (Orbit shows which of our accounts to add as a
collaborator), a repository transferred from us to you, or a download.
